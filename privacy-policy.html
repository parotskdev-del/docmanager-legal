Privacy Policy

DocManager by ParotskDev

Effective September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the DocManager mobile application handles user and device data.

Your documents stay on your device

Everything you scan, import, or create in DocManager — including images, PDFs, document names, folder names, and descriptions you type — is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it. We do not upload this content to ourselves or anyone else. Uninstalling DocManager removes it.

When you share or export

When you share or export a document, it is provided to the app you choose, such as a messenger, mail client, or file manager. From that point, that app's privacy practices apply rather than this Privacy Policy.

Features not included in this build

Cloud sync, AI summaries, and content search are planned features. They are not available in this build, and DocManager does not upload your documents anywhere.

What we collect

DocManager collects limited pseudonymous usage statistics and crash diagnostics through Google Firebase. A usage event records which screen was opened or which action finished. It contains only counts, durations, yes/no flags, and values from a fixed list defined in advance. For example, an event may state that a four-page document was exported as a PDF and that the export took 1.2 seconds.

Firebase also associates reports with a randomly generated identifier for this app installation, device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and the country Google derives from your IP address. Crash reports additionally contain a stack trace and the app state at the time of the crash.

What never leaves your device

Document contents, document names, folder names, file paths, and text you type are never included in usage or crash reports. They are not sent to us or Google when collection is enabled or in any other circumstance.

Why we collect it

We use usage statistics to understand which features people use and crash diagnostics to find and fix failures. We do not sell or share this data, show advertising, or use advertising identifiers. DocManager removes the advertising-ID permission that the analytics library would otherwise add.

Who processes it

Google processes this data for us through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. It is processed on Google's infrastructure under Google's terms. We do not provide this data to any other party.

How long it is kept

Usage events are retained for 2 months. Data associated with an app installation is retained for 14 months. Crash reports are retained for 90 days.

Your choice

In DocManager, go to Settings → Privacy & legal → Usage data. One switch controls both usage statistics and crash reports. Turning it off stops future collection immediately and it remains off until you turn it on again. Turning it off does not delete reports already sent. To request deletion of data already collected, email parotsk.dev@gmail.com.
